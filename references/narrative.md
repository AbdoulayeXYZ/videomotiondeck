# Narrative — the story structure that wins

A pitch video is not a slideshow with a voice. It is a short film with one job: make one specific audience
(a jury, a partner, an investor) **want to say yes**. Every second either moves them toward that yes or wastes it.

## The 8 beats (3:00 film — scale proportionally)

| # | Beat | Time | What it does | Typical scenes |
|---|------|------|--------------|----------------|
| 1 | **Cold open** | 0:00–0:12 | A time, a place, an image. No logo, no "hello". Drop them into the moment. | `clip` / `still` + caption, `title` |
| 2 | **Stakes** | 0:12–0:30 | The problem in one brutal sentence + one sourced number. | `title`, `stat` |
| 3 | **The human** | 0:30–0:45 | One named person who suffers the problem (driver, nurse, farmer…). Give them a name and a detail. | `still`/`clip` + caption |
| 4 | **The question** | 0:45–0:55 | Turn the problem into a question the film will answer. It becomes the callback at the end. | `question` |
| 5 | **The brief + the answer** | 0:55–1:10 | Who asked (the brief owner, by name) → "Voici notre réponse." → product name. | `brief` (card → answer) |
| 6 | **How it works + proof** | 1:10–1:50 | 3–4 capabilities max, each one a benefit; the platform; 1–2 numbers. | `trio`, `features`, `screen`, `stat` |
| 7 | **Why YOU win** (the partner) | 1:50–2:35 | The longest act when a company submitted the brief: their clients, their teams, their revenue, their risk. Then a concrete, low-risk next step (pilot). | `loop`, `network`, `steps` |
| 8 | **Team + close** | 2:35–3:00 | Faces and names (readable!), then the callback answer + "Merci." | `team`, `logo` |

Proportions that worked: problem+human ≈ 25 %, solution ≈ 25 %, partner value + proposal ≈ 30 %, team+close ≈ 15 %.

## Lessons from a real jury (AI-Karangué, URBAN SKILLS hackathon — see case_study_ai_karangue.md)

The first cut (4:45) was beautiful and was still sent back. The jury said:

1. **"Max 3 minutes."** A time cap is a hard constraint, not a guideline. → `max_duration`, the `plan` word budget, QC fails if over.
2. **"End-of-slide pauses make us think the video is over."** Silence + still picture = the end, in the viewer's brain.
   → the voice drives the cut: `tighten` trims every segment to its last word + 0.55 s; QC flags voice gaps > 1.8 s.
3. **"No repetitions — we saw the same platform screens several times."** Each image must carry a new idea.
   → `plan` warns when a file is used twice. Same for sentences: never say the same thing twice in different words.
4. **"It's our brief — talk about us much more. Say we submitted it."** The brief owner is the hero of act 7.
   → "Cette question, c'est Oh Garage qui nous l'a posée." + 3 segments on what *they* gain.
5. **"Too commercial."** Selling features to a partner sounds like a vendor. Show their gain and propose a pilot.
   → No price grid, no feature dump. "Vos garages installent. Vos techniciens entretiennent. Notre intelligence veille."
6. **"Show why we should invest."** Translate the product into *their* business: new revenue line, customer loyalty,
   recurring maintenance, data, image. Then de-risk: pilot on their assets, they test, they validate, then joint roll-out.
7. **"Give people time to read the names."** Team scene: ≥ 2 s per person + 3 s, no voice over it (`voice_free`).

## Rules of thumb

- **One idea per segment.** If you need "and" to describe a segment, split it.
- **Callback.** The question of beat 4 returns at the end with its answer ("Qui veille sur Moussa ? Désormais, nous.").
- **Concrete beats abstract.** "Il est 3 h du matin, quelque part à Dakar" > "La somnolence est un problème majeur".
- **Numbers: few, sourced, rounded.** "745 vies par an" + source on screen. Never invent a figure; if it is an
  objective or an illustration, say so on screen.
- **Partner vocabulary.** "vous / vos" in act 7. Their client segments by name (fleets, embassies, State…).
- **End on the relationship, not on the product.** Logo × partner logo, tagline, "Merci."
- **Never fabricate** letters of intent, partner logos you were not allowed to use, quotes, screenshots of a product
  that does not exist (a clearly-labelled mock-up is fine).

## Adapting the structure

| Situation | Change |
|-----------|--------|
| No brief owner (open hackathon, investors) | Act 7 becomes "the market + business model + traction"; keep the pilot/next step. |
| Product demo for customers | Shorter acts 2–4 (30 s), act 6 longer with `screen` segments of the real product. |
| 60–90 s teaser | ~150–200 words, 8–11 segments: 1+3 merged (a named person in a place), 2 (one stat), 4, 5 (brief → answer), 6 (`trio` or `features` + one `screen`/`stat`), **7 kept** when someone submitted the brief (one `loop` or `steps`: their gain + pilot), 8 (`logo`; `team` only with real names). Budget: words ≈ (cap − 8 s) × 2.6. |
| Internal / institutional | Replace "why you win" with "why it matters for the mission" + roadmap `steps`. |
